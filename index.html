Your skin, over time.

From individual scans to a report you can return to.

Make a month of scans useful.

The report answers three questions: how is my skin today, is it getting better, and what is behind the numbers?

Different histories, one report

Noor has a month of scans. Jordan has one. Sam has gaps and missing details.

The brief’s fictional profiles guided these scenarios.

Design choices ↓

Start with today. Add context over time.

Give scores a baseline. Show change from the first scan.

Compare the same area. Keep trends and photos comparable.

Show the dates. Place products and events alongside scores without claiming cause.

Information architecture

At a glance · Products · Trends · Calendar · Photos

Scan details and the monthly recap open from the report. The report uses observations, not diagnoses.

User flows ↓

User flows.

The report opens with your latest score. Explore a section, open a detail, or revisit the month.

The report sections are tabs, so people can switch between them at any time.

Open report → Score reveal → Report page
  • At a glanceScore card → metric trend → select a day → scan detailsLatest scan → scan details
  • Monthly highlights → Monthly recapBrowse the story
  • Products → Product detailsExpand routine and product cards
  • Progress → Score trendsSelect an area, score and day
  • Calendar → Choose a daySelect a day and open a scan
  • Photos → Compare photosChange light or area and open a scan
  • Ask Lumi AI → Lumi AI sheetAvailable from every report tab

On first load, the score reveal advances into the report; tap to skip. It is skipped for reduced motion. At a glance opens by default; preview score cards are not interactive.
